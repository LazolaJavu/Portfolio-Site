About me

Building with data,
thinking with statistics

I’m passionate about exploring new ideas and I like to think of myself as an early adopter. That curiosity comes from a deep love for problem-solving — a passion that really started when I got to varsity. I fell in love with math and physics because they gave me something powerful: the freedom to play with complex problems, but within clear rules. Exploring different approaches, testing ideas, and finding better solutions is where I thrive.

I also enjoy building systems that automate boring, repetitive tasks — not because I hate the work, but because I’d rather free up time for learning, experimenting, and solving more interesting problems. That mindset has taken me on many new journeys, and over the past few years it’s helped me make real strides in my workplace by delivering impactful projects and building tools that are genuinely useful.

I enjoy collaborating with others because it often puts me in rooms where I don’t know everything — and that’s exactly where I want to be. It pushes me to grow, learn faster, and see problems from different perspectives.

My love for data science comes from two things: first, it’s famously known as the “sexiest job of the 21st century” (I didn’t make the rules, I just accepted the title). Second, it’s an ever-evolving field that constantly challenges you to learn, adapt, and improve — and that excites me.

If you’re viewing my portfolio, feel free to reach out through the About Me page. Let me know what you’re looking for and how we can help each other. I’m always open to exploring new opportunities — although fair warning: I can be a little picky sometimes. Not because the work isn’t exciting, but because I’m intentional about the direction I’m building toward.
